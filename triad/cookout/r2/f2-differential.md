## Replay of accepted trial dishes (honest cost of the head-noun rule)

708 distinct accepted dishes from trial runs 2-7 were replayed through this scan: **63 (8.9%) would now be rejected** once and need a repair. They are the intended price of the rule: the chef used a picked product's modifier word as a food of its own (peanuts for peanut flour, black beans for black bean sauce, cranberry for cranberry sauce, orange for orange juice, lime for lime cordial, strawberry for strawberry sauce/ice cream, chicken for a chicken carcass...). Another ~15 flags are old cases where no pick supplies the word (hen's egg, maccheroni, wasabi root, cep, prickly pear, green cardamom pods, scotch egg sausagemeat): those were accepted before only through family exemptions and bare-word cover.

Changes that keep natural prose passing: trailing pack words (pot, jar, pouch, tray, whole) and cut/form words (powder, flakes, dough, breast, mince, cloves...) are looked through to the food behind them; a pick's own name shortened by the chef at either end ("pepperoni pizza" for frozen pepperoni pizza, "dried chilli" for dried chillies whole) is the pick; names whose first word changes the food (sea cucumber, bone marrow, water spinach, chocolate orange) never cover the bare last word.

Not re-trialled: the prompts are unchanged. With 63 of 708 dishes now needing a repair, about one basket in four (1 - 0.911^3) would need at least one repair run instead of about one in six before; a fresh 40-basket trial would measure whether the first-attempt bar (30 of 40) still holds. Offered, not run.
