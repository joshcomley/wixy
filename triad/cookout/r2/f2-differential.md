## Follow-ups found while classifying (fixed, with tests)

- The picked-food crisps-subject rule (v3 L3) let "salt and vinegar crisps" / "beef and onion crisps" through when a vinegar or onion was picked ("and" counted as a conjunction before the subject). Only until/till/as/once/when/so/that now count.
- A generic pasta pick allowed any pasta shape, even for a specific shape pick ("elbow macaroni" from angel hair pasta). Only picks that are not themselves a shape (lentil, beetroot, saffron pasta...) allow shapes.
- Two picks combine into a phrase only when its last food word is a cook's compound base (`POOLED_HEADS`: butter, oil, jam, curd, syrup...). "Duck egg", "salt cod", "crab apple", "milk chocolate" are no longer shorthand.
