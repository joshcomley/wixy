# A ringing or connecting call pauses the idle lock

**Symptom.** Tapping Call and not being answered within ~10 s of the last touch idle-locked the chat and dropped the call; the same hit the device being called.

**Decision (operator #2136, 2026-10-28: "Yes, keep it unlocked while ringing").** A device holds the single `voiceCall` idle suspension from the moment it sends an invite (or shows an incoming call) through ringing, connecting and connected/muted. `holdCall()` in `admin-ui/src/server/call/controller.ts` takes it (idempotent, so ringing flows into connected with no gap); `teardownCall` is the only release, so every end path (decline, cancel, ring timeout, setup timeout, failure, socket close, detach, dispose) restarts a fresh countdown.

**Why it is safe.** The server already bounds an attempt: ringing at most 30 s, connecting at most 20 s (`call_broker.py`, unchanged); the client also ends at 30 s / 20 s. The pause therefore cannot outlive a real attempt.

**Still locks immediately.** Escape, panic, multi-tap, tab-hidden/screen-lock policies, token expiry and 401; every actual lock/detach still ends the call and its audio. Only the automatic *idle* lock is affected; device grants unchanged. `voiceCall` is not in `isPickerOrMicOpen`.

**Round 2 (audit findings F2 high, F1 low).** (F2) A page frozen mid-ring (phone auto-lock suspends JS) left the plain hold in place: `idleRanOutWhileAway()` returned false while any suspension existed, and the late release re-armed a FRESH idle window, so the hidden chat showed to whoever woke the phone (with both "lock when I leave" boxes unticked). Connected calls had the same hole already (the hold predates this change), now fixed too. The `voiceCall` hold and the call's setup `micPermission` hold are now liveness-stamped (`hooks.suspendLive` -> `touch()`/`release()`); the controller's 100 ms tick touches them; stale after `LIVE_HOLD_STALE_MS` = 3 s (three missed beats of a throttled hidden tab at 1 s, far below the 10 s idle). Stale holds are ignored by the away check (silence counts from the last heartbeat), and a stale release/touch locks at once or resumes the countdown from the last heartbeat. Other suspensions (recording, mediaPlaying, filePicker, viewOnce, goodnight, adventureAnimating, the recorder's micPermission) stay plain: they are bounded by a visible foreground UI or OS-held audio, and changing them is outside this decision; recorded as a known residual. (F1) A callee stops taking the ring hold after 2 consecutive unanswered incoming rings (ended by the caller, server or ring timeout) with no change in `hooks.activityCount()` between; answering still holds the connecting/connected call. Residual: a peer can still keep a device unlocked for two rings (~60 s) per touch.

**Watch for.** Any new call state must still pass through `teardownCall`; a path that clears `callId`/`outgoingRequest` without it would leak the pause (tests in `tests/server/call.test.ts` cover each end).
